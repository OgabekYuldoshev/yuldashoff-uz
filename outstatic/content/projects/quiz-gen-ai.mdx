QuizGenAI is an innovative platform that leverages AI to help users create personalized quizzes and tests effortlessly. Users can upload various content types like PDFs, DOCX, or TXT files, and the platform generates relevant questions based on the content. The system not only generates quizzes but also allows users to edit, save, and download test results in PDF format, making it a powerful tool for educators and learners alike.
